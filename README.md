## A short story

The lighthouse keeper had kept the same light for forty years, and every night he climbed the spiral stairs without fail. One evening his granddaughter arrived unannounced, carrying a radio and a question: why bother, when ships had satellites now? He thought about it while the lamp warmed. "Because somewhere a sailor I'll never meet is counting on a steady light," he said. She didn't answer. She just sat beside him and watched the beam sweep across the dark water, one slow turn at a time.
